Our approach

Systems-level.
By design.

We go below the UI layer. JNI, Win32, AccessibilityService, null-routing VPNs. The kind of work most teams outsource or skip.

⚙️

Systems-Level Depth

JNI bridges to llama.cpp for local LLM inference. Win32 process killing via JNA. Android AccessibilityService for instant OS interception. No UI-only abstractions.

🔒

Real Enforcement

Soft timers are not enforcement. We use Device Admin mode, null-routing on-device VPNs, Windows Firewall rules, and SHA-256 native PINs. Bypassing our apps takes real effort.

📴

Zero Cloud by Default

Our AI runs on your GPU. Your relationship data stays on your phone. Our VPNs null-route locally — no packet ever reaches our servers. Privacy is the architecture, not a setting.
